# PeaceTech

PeaceTech is a trauma-aware AI support and analytics platform designed to combine journaling, emotional insight, dashboard visibility, and future health-data integrations into a more human-centered support system.

## Overview

PeaceTech is built to support veterans and underserved users through a product experience that blends reflection tools, insight layers, and dashboard-based visibility.

## Problem

Many support systems are reactive, fragmented, or impersonal. PeaceTech is designed to create a more continuous and emotionally aware experience through journaling, insight generation, and future connected data workflows.

## Who It’s For

- Veterans and underserved users needing support tools
- Partners evaluating trauma-aware AI solutions
- Future healthcare, pilot, or dashboard stakeholders

## Current Status

Working prototype / platform concept in active development

## Core Features

- PeaceTech landing page

- Trauma-aware support and risk-scoring direction
- Emotional insight and dashboard presentation layer
